ICO PC

ICO, the PAL PlayStation 2 game, as a normal program for Windows, Linux, the Steam Deck, Android, iPhone and Mac. It runs the game's own code, so it plays like the PS2 game, with a sharper picture, widescreen and smoother motion when you want them.
