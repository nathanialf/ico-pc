## New

- Options > Picture: Gamma, Picture brightness, Contrast and Saturation now change by 1 with each press instead of 5.
- The log now checks the characters' bone data on the graphics chip every tenth of a second and says when a value came back wrong, for a Turnip driver report.

## Fixed

- Entering a test room from Options > Extras > Unused content no longer ends in a game over at once: the character starts on the room's floor in both rooms.
- Motion blur: Smooth made the picture far too blurry when the camera moved at Frame rate 60 or Uncapped on some devices; the trail is now as long as the original's at every frame rate.
