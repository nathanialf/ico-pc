## New

- Menus: every menu, the game's and the port's own, now uses one clean font drawn in the original menus' style (light letters, dark outline, the same soft grain and the glow on the chosen line), so all menus match in every language. The first start no longer reads the game's lettering from the disc.
- Android: the picture reaches into the notch and fills folding screens when they open.
- Android: smoother play (fewer wasted frames), and a Resolution setting, Auto, that lowers itself when the phone falls behind.
- Characters: Options > Extras > Characters lets you recolour Ico's skin, poncho (four colour groups), tunic and shorts, and Yorda's skin and dress, or roll random colours; from the title screen, Cross on a row shows the character in the viewer. In the pause menu, Extras now has just this page.
