## New

- Changing a gamepad button under Remap controls no longer changes the menus: the bottom button always chooses and the top button always goes back, whatever you set for play.
- Aspect ratio Auto now follows screens wider than 32:9, and 48:9 is in the list.
- Resolution goes up to 16x, and on wide screens the picture is no longer held to 4K. If your graphics card cannot hold the size you chose, the game lowers it on its own and the row shows the size in use.

## Fixed

- Full pixel (no border) now shows the whole picture the game draws, at its own sharpness. Before, it enlarged the finished picture, which cut a little off the sides and made it softer.
- Giving a gamepad button to another action now swaps the two instead of leaving one with no button, so the Options menu can no longer become unreachable from the pad.

## Known issues

- At the old bridge save bench, a dark patch on the floor in the back corner flickers while the camera turns.
- Distant water surfaces shimmer while the camera moves.
