**Please note:** this release adds four more discs the game can play and has had limited testing on them (automated checks and one start to the first scene of each, plus the rc checks described in the notes). If something looks or plays wrong, keep the previous version and send the game's report file (the newest file in the `logs` folder that starts with `report-`, or `ico-pc.log`) with a short description of what you saw, and say which disc you played: the European disc (SCES-50760), the Japanese disc (SCPS-11003), the Korean disc (SCPS-56001), the North American disc (SCUS-97113), the Asian disc (SCPS-55001), the August 2001 preview disc or the June 2001 magazine demo (SCUS-97158).

## New

- **The North American disc of ICO** (SCUS-97113, `Ico_USA.iso`) plays, from the same download on every device. It is in English, confirms with Cross, and runs at 60 Hz only.
- **The August 2001 preview disc** (`Ico_AUG6.iso`), the early version of the game that came before the North American release, plays too. It shares the North American disc's save folder, as the two did on a PS2.
- **The Asian disc of ICO** (SCPS-55001, `Ico_CN.iso`) plays too. The game's own menus are in Chinese, and so are the menus this port adds (Options and the extras): the Menu language row starts on Chinese (繁體中文, Traditional Chinese) with this disc and can be changed to any other language.
- **The June 2001 magazine demo disc** (SCUS-97158, `Ico_JUN26.iso`), the playable ICO demo from the Official U.S. PlayStation Magazine's demo disc 49, an early version made before the August preview, plays too. It needs the whole disc image, not only ICO's files. Setting it up keeps only ICO's part of the disc (about 280 MB, none of the other games on it) and checks that part instead of reading the whole 4.4 GB image, so the first start is much quicker. On Android the 4.4 GB image is first copied into the app, which needs about 5.6 GB free and takes a few minutes; the screen says so while it copies. The message shown when there is not enough space, and the one shown when the copy fails, now come in the menu language you chose under Options, when you have chosen one.
- **Game variants**, under Options > Extras (from the title screen or the pause menu, on every disc): change how the game plays. Shadow toughness (the disc's own, as on the North American disc, or not tougher on later playthroughs), One hit per swing, Shadows never dodge, Shadow aggression (bold or wary, patient or quick), No hints, Queen barrier (5 or 7 hits), No cutscene look-around, Ignore cutscene cameras (cutscenes can look wrong), and, with Developer mode on, the game's own Debug menu. Everything starts as your disc shipped it. One hit per swing, Shadows never dodge, Ignore cutscene cameras and the Debug menu switch achievements off while they are on; the other rows leave them on.
- On the June 2001 magazine demo, Options > Extras > Credits does not play: the demo disc has no ending credits. One secret achievement cannot be earned on the North American disc, the August 2001 preview or the June 2001 demo, since those versions of the game do not have what it asks for.

## Fixed

- On phones, tablets and Macs, the thin dotted lines of mist along the seams of walls and floors above the original resolution are now filled in, as on other computers.
- With MSAA anti-aliasing on, hairline gaps along walls and floors no longer show as dotted lines of mist.
- With MSAA anti-aliasing on, small openings onto the sky no longer glow brighter or wider than with it off.
- MSAA anti-aliasing finishes each picture once instead of twice in scenes with shadows, so it costs less.
- The puddle ripples no longer slide against their reflection while the camera moves.
