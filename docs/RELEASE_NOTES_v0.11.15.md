## New

- Options > Controls > Zoom type: choose Toggle to press the zoom button (R2) once to zoom the camera in and again to zoom it out, instead of holding it.

## Fixed

- An attempted fix for short freezes in the title and menus on some Android phones after a slow picture, when the game made its background copy.
- An attempted fix for the stutter in the boat scene on phones like the moto g24: the skipping of tiny pieces of the picture added in the previous version did not help there, so the game now times each area's pictures with it and without it while you play and keeps it only in the areas where it measured faster; an area it has not timed yet follows the last area it timed (with it before any).
