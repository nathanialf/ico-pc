## New

(kept current after every merge; player words only)

- Each save keeps the difficulty its journey was started on, on either memory card; a save from an earlier version keeps the gameplay options you had chosen, from the first time you load it.
- Difficulty modes: choose Easy, Normal or Hard when you start a new game, or change every rule yourself under Customize, including three new ones: a limit on how often Ico can be knocked down, No Shadows, and Permadeath (any game over ends the journey). Each save keeps its own choice. The difficulty rows have moved from Settings to the New Game screen. From the pause menu, Settings > Gameplay > Difficulty shows what the journey was started with, and the pause screen's journey lines show the mode and the knock-down count.
- A knock-down limit for a journey: the game is over once Ico has been knocked down a chosen number of times, in one room or over the whole journey (three in each room on Hard). He lies for two seconds first and cannot be hit meanwhile; a stumble at a ledge does not count.
- Save states remember the journey's difficulty: loading one brings back the difficulty and the knock-down count it was saved with, and a save state made with an earlier version is not refused for its difficulty settings (it takes the difficulty it was made with). On a Permadeath journey save states are switched off: none can be saved or loaded.
- No Shadows, a choice on a new journey's Customize page: no Shadow appears anywhere, and the story goes on as if you had won each fight, the cage room and the final hall included.

## Fixed

(kept current after every merge; player words only)

- The game no longer hitches when it saves a diagnostic capture; on phones the pictures it saves automatically for a report are written in the background, and the Settings bug-report picture now brings the newest of them along, so one attachment is enough.
- A crash on older Intel graphics (6th to 10th generation, on Intel's last drivers for them) when raising the resolution while playing.
