## New

- The mouse moves the camera while you play, and in photo mode, with the pointer hidden. Options > Controls has Mouse camera, Mouse sensitivity and Invert mouse up/down (issue 17).
- The mouse pointer works in every menu, the game's and the port's own (issue 17).
- Window mode: Windowed, Borderless or Fullscreen. Alt+Enter switches between Windowed and the other mode you last used (issue 17).
- Options > Effects > Cinematic bars turns off the black bars of cutscenes (issue 27).
- Android: Options > Graphics driver lets you add a graphics driver of your own on phones with an Adreno chip (issue 22).

## Fixed

- Bright dots along seams at higher resolutions are gone (issue 26).
- Android: Continue on the title screen no longer closes the game (issue 20).
- Android: Ico's and Yorda's arms should no longer stretch straight up when they hold hands (issue 19).
