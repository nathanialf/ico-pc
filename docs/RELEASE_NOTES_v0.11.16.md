## New

- Save states now load whatever your settings are: a state made with other settings (Hold type, Zoom type, Camera reset, the Gameplay rows and the rest) used to be refused, and now loads with the settings you have now. A state made in the other of 50 Hz and 60 Hz plays at its own until you restart the game.
- Options > Controls > Look at Yorda: choose Hold or Toggle to turn Ico and the camera toward Yorda with L3 (click the left stick in) without calling her, so she stays where she is.
- The log's summary of where each scene's time went now also compares each part of the picture with the graphics chip's speeds measured at start-up, and says roughly how much of each part's time those speeds account for.

## Fixed

- An attempted fix for the slowdown in the boat scene and the cage scene on phones like the moto g24: a start-up check of the faster way of drawing scenery close to the camera wrongly failed on these phones, so that drawing was never used there; the check now prepares everything it compares before it starts, and the game times that way of drawing on its own while you play (never on the title screen) and keeps it only where it is faster.
- An attempted fix for the slowdown in busy scenes such as the boat scene on phones like the moto g24: the scenery's shapes that cannot be seen are skipped again on these phones, in a safer way than the one that turned the picture black there before, and only after a start-up check that draws a scene of real size and confirms every picture comes out the same; if that check fails, if a picture comes back unfinished while it is in use, or if the game ends badly while using it, it stays off.
- An attempted fix for the slowdown in busy scenes such as the boat and the cage on phones like the moto g24: most see-through scenery is drawn once instead of twice where a check at start-up shows the game that this phone gives exactly the same picture that way, the scenery close to the camera only where that part of the check passes on its own, and the game times it while you play and keeps it only where it is faster.
- An attempted fix for the slowdown in busy scenes such as the boat and the cage on phones like the moto g24: some see-through scenery away from the camera is drawn once instead of twice where a check at start-up shows the game that this phone gives exactly the same picture that way, and the game times it while you play and keeps it only where it is faster.
